Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term option to replace missing teeth. A small titanium post is placed in the jaw and later fitted with a crown that looks and works like a natural tooth.

This treatment is often suitable for adults who have lost one or more teeth and have adequate bone support. Your dentist will assess this during examination.

Treatment usually involves more than one visit: planning, placement, healing time, and the final crown. You will be guided on care at home between visits.

What to expect: a detailed check-up first, then a clear plan. Comfort is a priority; local anaesthesia is used when needed.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment is used when the soft tissue inside a tooth is inflamed or infected. The goal is to save your natural tooth when possible.

It is often recommended for toothache, deep decay, or infection. Your dentist will confirm if this is the right option for you.

Many cases are completed in one or a few visits, depending on the tooth and infection. A crown may be advised afterwards to protect the tooth.

What to expect: the area is numbed for a more comfortable experience. Mild sensitivity after treatment can occur and is discussed at your visit.

Braces & Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment helps straighten teeth and improve bite. Options may include fixed braces or other appliances based on your needs.

It can help teenagers and adults who want better alignment for comfort, cleaning, or appearance.

Treatment length varies—often several months to a few years—with regular adjustment visits. Your plan is personalised after examination.

What to expect: records and photos may be taken first. You will receive advice on cleaning and what foods to limit during treatment.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening can lighten tooth colour that has darkened with time, diet, or staining. It is different from home DIY kits.

It may suit adults with healthy teeth and gums who want a brighter smile. Not every stain type responds the same way.

Often completed in a clinic session or a short series of visits, depending on the method chosen for you.

What to expect: sensitivity can occur temporarily. Results and suitability are confirmed after examination. Treatment plan and cost are confirmed after examination.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns cover and protect damaged teeth. Bridges replace missing teeth by using neighbouring teeth or implants for support.

They are often used after large fillings, root canal treatment, fracture, or tooth loss.

Treatment typically needs at least two visits: preparation and fitting of the final restoration. Temporary crowns may be used in between.

What to expect: shade and shape are matched as closely as possible to your other teeth. Care instructions are given after fitting.

General Dentistry

General dentistry includes checkups, scaling (cleaning), fillings, and extractions when a tooth cannot be saved.

It is for the whole family—routine care and treatment of common dental problems.

A checkup is often a single visit; fillings or extractions depend on the tooth. Scaling may be advised periodically for gum health.

What to expect: a gentle examination, explanation of findings, and a plan you can understand. Comfort measures are used when treatment is needed.
